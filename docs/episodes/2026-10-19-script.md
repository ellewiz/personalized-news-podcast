## Tech and AI

Apple's new smart home display, HomeView, is shaping up as a lesson in how long a finished product can sit waiting on its software. Mark Gurman, the Bloomberg reporter who's tracked this project for two years, says finished units have been sitting boxed up in warehouses for close to a year. The holdup wasn't the hardware. It was Apple's AI assistant, Siri AI, which only shipped last month. Apple reportedly pushed the launch back repeatedly, from a planned spring 2025 debut all the way to last week's unveiling. The device itself runs on an older A18 chip with 8 gigabytes of memory, specs that suggest it was designed years before it reached shelves. Daring Fireball, the Apple-focused tech blog, also flagged an earlier report from The Information claiming HomeView would ship with a robotic swiveling base. That never happened, and the blog argues it's further proof that vague sourcing in tech reporting often gets ahead of the facts. Separately, a blogger named Manton Reece floated a theory for why Apple priced the wall-mounted HomeView the same as the version with a built-in subwoofer base. His guess is that Apple wants to discourage people from choosing the cheaper-feeling option unless they're certain they want to drill into a wall.
