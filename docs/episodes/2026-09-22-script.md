## Markets

The metal pared some of its losses as oil swung on the shifting supply picture, with investors trying to figure out how cheaper energy costs might shape the Federal Reserve's next moves on interest rates. Lower oil prices generally ease inflation pressure, which gives the Fed more room to cut rates, and that's the calculation gold traders are running right now.
