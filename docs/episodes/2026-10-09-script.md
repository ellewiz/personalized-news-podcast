## Markets

That strength is coinciding with a broader shift toward caution. The company reiterated that stance in a post on X on Friday, rejecting any suggestion the firings were retaliatory.

The Jaguars, for their part, are in the middle of an unusual stretch of their own. They're set to play 26 of their next 30 regular-season games away from Jacksonville, which would make them the first NFL team since 1926 to play in a dozen or more stadiums in a single season.

## the WNBA

Analysts are already placing her performance alongside the greatest runs in pro sports history, not just basketball. But Wilson isn't dwelling on the trophy. She said she'll skip the pregame ceremony to collect it because she wants her focus on Friday's Game 3 against Golden State.

That game matters because the Aces are fighting for their season. The Valkyries, Golden State's second-year franchise, took a 2-0 series lead behind Veronica Burton's go-ahead three-pointer in the final minute of Game 2. Tiffany Hayes, a 37-year-old veteran, has been central to that surge, and Golden State now sits one win away from its first WNBA Finals appearance.
