## the NFL

The Philadelphia Eagles are in a five-game losing streak, the longest of head coach Nick Sirianni's tenure, and the fallout from Monday night's loss to the Cowboys is still being sorted out. The Eagles dropped to two and five after a defensive performance that Bleeding Green Nation called the most frustrating of the Vic Fangio era. Defensive linemen Jalen Carter and Jordan Davis played heavy snap counts but produced little to show for it. Sirianni addressed the struggles afterward, including how the defense handled Saquon Barkley's return and how the offense got going early. Reports also indicate the Eagles were among several teams, along with the Packers and Bears, that showed interest in Colts guard Quenton Nelson before he was traded elsewhere. With the team now projected among the worst in football, one outlet notes Philadelphia currently sits in the top six of the 2027 draft order. The Eagles also trimmed their practice squad this week, releasing two players.

Elsewhere, the Indianapolis Colts blew up their roster in a single day. They traded defensive tackle DeForest Buckner back to the San Francisco 49ers, the team that originally drafted him, in exchange for a 2027 fourth-round pick. They also sent guard Quenton Nelson, an eight-time Pro Bowler, to the Seattle Seahawks, who waived guard Christian Haynes to make room for him. Analysts framed both deals as the clearest signal yet that Indianapolis is beginning a rebuild, with speculation now turning to whether running back Jonathan Taylor could be moved next. One report suggested the New York Jets could be a long-term beneficiary of the Colts' selloff, even though Seattle and San Francisco are the ones cashing in immediately.

There's injury news across the league. Green Bay Packers edge rusher Micah Parsons may return to practice as soon as next week, more than ten months after knee surgery, and head coach Matt LaFleur said the team is hopeful. Chicago Bears defensive end Montez Sweat is back with the team after serving a two-game suspension for violating the NFL's performance-enhancing substance policy. He said he's simply moving forward from it. Baltimore Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson is expected to return Sunday after missing three games with a high ankle sprain. The Ravens also have rookie guard Olaivavega Ioane expected to miss time with an MCL sprain suffered in their loss to the Bengals. The New York Giants are placing running back Tyrone Tracy Junior on injured reserve with a hamstring injury, meaning Devin Singletary should see a bigger workload. Lions quarterback Jared Goff backed head coach Dan Campbell's decision to go for it on fourth down deep in their own territory during a tie game, even though Gibbs was stopped for a loss on the play.

## the WNBA

The Atlanta Dream spent part of this week fighting for a place to play their own home game. The team told the city that concerns about finding an available Finals venue for Game 6 went unheeded, calling the situation, in a letter, "not what we fought for." The issue got resolved by moving up Thursday's tipoff, now set for 12:30 in the afternoon Eastern time, so the Dream could share State Farm Arena with the Hawks.

That early start means Game 6 of the WNBA Finals tips off Thursday with the Golden State Valkyries holding a three-games-to-two lead over Atlanta. A win for Golden State closes out the series and hands the Valkyries their first title.

Much of the credit for that lead is going to Kiah Stokes, the Valkyries center. She's been playing both ends of the floor, anchoring Golden State's defense while also contributing offensively, and analysts covering the series point to her as a central reason Golden State is one win away from a championship.

The series has also drawn star power off the court. Zendaya and Megan Rapinoe, the retired U.S. women's national soccer team star, were both spotted at a sold-out Game 5 at Ballhalla, as the Dream and Valkyries played in front of a packed house.

Looking further ahead, ESPN released its first mock draft for the WNBA's 2027 class. JuJu Watkins is projected to go first overall, with Madison Booker and Hannah Hidalgo rounding out the top three picks.
